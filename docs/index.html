New session from context

Never re-explain what you just did.

Select a few cards and start a session from their context. You get a handoff of what each one did, edit it if you like, say what's next, and the new session picks up from there.

  • ⇧click cards to select them
  • ⌘↩start it

Agents and messages

See how your sessions work together.

When Claude runs agents, they peek out from under its card in their status colours. Point at the deck to fan them out, or click one for its live transcript. When two sessions message each other, a dotted line joins their cards and every message runs along it as a light.

Whiteboard

Sketch it out, with Claude beside you.

Draw with the pen, drop in shapes, stickers and pictures, and link anything to anything with arrows that follow it around. Claude sees the board too: ask it to lay out a plan or link what's related, and it draws right next to your work.

  • Ppen
  • Rshape
  • Alink two things
  • Ssticker

Frames and groups

Keep related work together.

Select a few things and put a frame round them. Claude names the frame, and everything in it moves together. A group glues cards, notes and shapes in place without asking Claude at all.

  • ⌥⌘Gframe them
  • ⌘Ggroup them

Views

Any view you need, just by asking.

Describe what you want to keep an eye on, like your tests, your last deploys or your open tickets. Claude builds it as a live page on the board, in Nodus's own look, and keeps it fresh. Ask, and it adds buttons that start a session, like Fix beside a failing test.

  • /viewdescribe a new one
  • ✎ask Claude to change it
  • Fixstart a session from it

Archive

Nothing gets lost.

Finished sessions stack up in one pile instead of cluttering the board. Hover it and type to search, then drag a card back out to pick the session up again.

Search

Which session touched that?

Search what every session said and did, live or archived: your prompts, Claude's replies, the files it edited and the commands it ran. Jump to the card, or pick a few results and start a new session from what they did about it.

  • ⌘Ksearch every session
  • ↩go to the card
  • ⇥pick results to start from

Focus

See only this week's projects.

Put the projects you're on this week in focus and flip the Focus switch. The projects page and the sidebar show only those, and the rest are one flip away. Nothing gets archived or moved, and a hidden project still shows while a session in it needs you.

  • Settingsturn on Focus mode
  • Right-clickAdd to Focus
  • ⇧⌘Fflip the switch

Floating capture

Start a session from any app.

Double-tap ⇧ wherever you are and a panel floats up over it. Type or say what you need, and Claude works out whether it's a new session, a note, a view or a reply to a session that's running, and which project it's for. The browser tab, a screenshot or what you copied can come along.

  • ⇧ ⇧open it anywhere
  • Tabchange where it goes
  • ↩send it

Server

Share a board with your team.

Run Nodus on a Linux server you own. Its projects show up in everyone's grid beside their own, sessions keep going while laptops sleep, and each card works on a branch of its own. Members sign in with GitHub and see each other's pointers on the board.

Also on the board

Ask Claude on the spot

Press B, click anywhere on the board and say what you need. Claude decides whether it's a session, a note or a view, and puts it right there.

Talk to Claude

Press the mic beside the prompt bar or in a chat, say what you want, and send it. It uses your own xAI key.

Action buttons

Describe a chore once, like “pull and push, resolving conflicts”. Claude writes the button, and a press runs it in the background.

Sticky notes

Pin reminders anywhere on the board, or ask Claude to research something and pin what it found beside its card.

Sessions outlive the app

Quit Nodus and Claude keeps working. Open it again and everything is right where you left it.

Native, written in Rust

Nodus draws its board on the GPU, so it opens fast and stays smooth with a busy board full of sessions.
